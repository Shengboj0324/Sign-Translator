# Information for reviewers

*Print in large type (16 pt or more). [Owner's name] or an interpreter will also explain everything here in ASL.*

---

## What is this project?

We are building a computer program that turns English sentences into ASL.
The program shows the signing with a **computer-made figure**, not a real person.
The figure has a plain face and a dark top, and it has no hair.
We need Deaf signers to tell us honestly whether the signing is clear and correct.
This is a research project. It is not for sale and makes no money.

## What will I do?

1. You watch short videos. Each one is a few seconds long and shows **one sign** or **one short sentence**.
2. You can watch each video as many times as you like.
3. For each video you answer a few simple questions on one page:
   - What was signed?
   - How clear was it?
   - Did it look natural?
   - Were the hands, the movement and the face right?
   - Anything else you want to tell us.
4. Some videos may be very good, some may be bad, and some may show almost nothing. **That is on purpose.**
   Please say honestly what you see. There are no wrong answers.

## How long does it take?

- About **30 to 45 minutes** for one session.
- You get a break about every 15 minutes, and you can ask for a break at any time.
- You can do it **on paper**, or tell your answers in ASL to [owner's name], who writes them down.

## Do I have to take part?

- **No.** Taking part is your choice.
- You can **stop at any time**, even in the middle, without giving a reason.
- You are still paid for the time you spent: [amount and how you are paid].

## What happens to my answers?

- Your **name is not on your answer pages**. You get a code number instead, for example R07.
- Only [owner's name] keeps the list that links your name to your code, in a private place.
- We use your answers to find out what the program does well and badly, and to improve it.
- Results are only shared as totals (for example "most reviewers found the signs clear").
  **Nobody can tell from them which answers are yours.**
- We do **not** film you, unless you agree separately on the consent form.
- You can ask us to delete your answers. Ask before [date], when the results are written up.
- Please do not share or copy the videos, and do not talk about them with other reviewers until everyone is finished.
