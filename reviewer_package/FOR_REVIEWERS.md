# Information for reviewers

*Print in large type (16 pt or more). [Owner's name] or an interpreter will also explain everything here in ASL.*

---

## What is this project?

We are building a computer program that turns English sentences into ASL.
Every video in this study shows the signing on a **computer-made figure**, not a person on camera.
The figure has a plain face and a dark top, and it has no hair.
We need Deaf signers to tell us honestly whether the signing is clear and correct.
This is a research project. It is not for sale and makes no money.

## What will I do?

1. You watch short videos. Each one is a few seconds long and shows **one short sentence**.
   You can watch each video as many times as you like.
2. **First part.** For each video you answer a few simple questions:
   - What was signed? Use English or gloss, whichever is easier. A guess is fine.
   - Was it a statement or a question?
   - Was the sentence negative?
   - Was a topic set up at the start?
   - Did it look natural?
   - Anything else you want to tell us.
3. **Second part.** After we have your answers to the first part, you see the videos again with four English sentences for each one, and you choose the sentence that was signed.
4. Some videos may be very good. Some may be hard to follow or poor, and some of that is **on purpose**.
   Please say honestly what you see. There are no wrong answers.

You do not need a computer for any of this. [Owner's name] plays the videos and handles every technical step. You give your answers **on paper**, or tell them in ASL to [owner's name], who writes them down.

## How long does it take?

- About **2.5 hours in all**: about 2 hours for the first part and about 30 minutes for the second. These are estimates, and consent, instructions and practice videos are on top.
- Sessions last up to about 45 minutes, so this is about [number of sessions] sessions. You can choose when to do them.
- You get a break about every 15 minutes, and you can ask for a break at any time.

## Do I have to take part?

- **No.** Taking part is your choice.
- You can **stop at any time**, even in the middle, without giving a reason.
- You are still paid for the time you spent: [amount and how you are paid].

## What happens to my answers?

- Your **name is not on your answer pages**. You get a code instead, for example R07. [Owner's name] makes the code and every other technical key.
- Only [owner's name] keeps the list that links your name to your code, in a private place.
- We use your answers to find out what the program does well and badly, and to improve it.
- Results are only shared as totals (for example "most reviewers found the signs clear").
  **Nobody can tell from them which answers are yours.**
- We do **not** film you, unless you agree separately on the consent form.
- You can ask us to delete your answers. Ask before [date], when the results are written up.
- Please do not share or copy the videos, and do not talk about them with other reviewers until everyone is finished.

## Where does the material come from?

- **How2Sign** (Duarte and others, CVPR 2021), shared under the CC BY-NC 4.0 licence. The English sentences come from it, and so does some of the motion behind the videos. We changed that material: the motion was estimated from recordings and is shown on the figure.
- **ASLLVD**, from the American Sign Language Linguistic Research Project (ASLLRP), Boston University, used under the ASLLRP Sign Bank Data Terms of Use. The program learned from it, and some of the motion may also come from it, changed in the same way.
