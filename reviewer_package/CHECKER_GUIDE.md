# Checker's guide: does this recording sign a negation?

*One page, for a session [owner's name] leads. Print in large type. [Owner's name] or an interpreter explains it in ASL. The owner's own steps are in `OPERATOR_GUIDE.md` §11.*

## Your job

Our study needs to know which sentences really are negative when signed. An English sentence can say "not" while the signer shows it another way, or not at all. You are the judge of that. You watch **real signers** in recorded video, one recording at a time.

You do not need a computer. [Owner's name] plays the recordings, writes down your answers and does everything technical.

## What we ask you not to do

- You never see any computer-made signing from this study. If you are shown any by mistake, please stop and tell [owner's name], because you could no longer do this job.
- Because you do this job, you cannot also rate videos in this study.
- Please do not discuss the recordings with anyone who may rate videos.

## How each recording goes

1. **Watch.** [Owner's name] plays one recording, with no words on the screen. Watch it as many times as you like.
2. **Say what you see.** Is a negation signed? It may be a sign such as NOT or NONE, a headshake across the sentence, or something else.
3. **Read.** Only then does [owner's name] show you the English sentence and the word in it that was flagged.
4. **Final answer.** Does this recording sign that negation: **yes** or **no**?
   - Judge the recording, not the English. If you cannot see the negation signed, the answer is no.
   - You may change your mind after reading. That is fine, and [owner's name] notes it.
   - If the clip seems cut off, or you are unsure, say so and [owner's name] notes it. Your yes or no is still needed.

## Time

About [number] recordings, at about 2 minutes each. You get a break about every 15 minutes, and you can ask for one at any time. You can stop at any time, and you are paid for the time you spent: [amount and how you are paid].
