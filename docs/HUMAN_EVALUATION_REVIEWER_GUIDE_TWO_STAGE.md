# Reviewer guide: judging ASL signing videos, in two stages

Thank you for reviewing. You may stop at any time. You are paid for the time you spend, including if you stop.

You do not need a computer for this, and you never have to do anything technical. You watch the videos. You give your answers on the printed form, or in ASL to [owner's name], who writes them down. [Owner's name] plays the videos, enters your answers and handles everything else.

You will work in two stages. Finish stage 1 before you get stage 2. Once you hand it in, stage 1 is final.

## How long it takes

This study has 60 videos. Stage 1 takes about 2 hours, at about 2 minutes per video. Stage 2 takes about 30 minutes. **That is about 2.5 hours in all,** in sessions of up to about 45 minutes. These are estimates. If your study has more videos, [owner's name] will tell you.

You get a break about every 15 minutes, and you can ask for one at any time.

## What you will see

A plain human figure in a dark top, seen from the front, signing. The figure is not a real person and it has no hair.

Its face is simple. The eyebrows are painted on and move with the skin above the eyes. The eyelids and eyes move. The mouth moves, but it has no separate lips. Some face signals may be weak or missing, so please tell us when the face looks wrong.

Some videos may be hard to follow or poor. Please judge each one on its own. You will not see the same sentence twice.

## Stage 1: watch and answer

Watch each video as many times as you need. Then answer on that video's page, or in ASL:

| Question | Your answer |
|---|---|
| Could you see it? | If the video does not play, or you cannot judge it at all, tick the box and skip the rest of that page. |
| What was signed? | Your own words, in English or in gloss, whichever is easier. A guess is fine. |
| What kind of sentence? | Statement, yes/no question, wh-question or rhetorical question |
| Was it negative? | Yes if the signed sentence is negative (for example a headshake or NOT), otherwise no |
| Was a topic set up? | Yes if a topic is set up at the start (for example with raised brows), otherwise no |
| Did it look natural? | 1 = not signing; 2 = hard to follow; 3 = understandable but unnatural; 4 = mostly natural; 5 = fluent and natural |
| Anything else? | What looked wrong, which sign was unclear, or what was missing on the face |

- Please answer every video, in order. If you need to stop, tell [owner's name]; the videos you have not reached stay empty, and you are still paid.
- The kind of sentence and the topic help us describe the signing. They are not graded in this study.
- Please work alone, and do not discuss the videos with other reviewers until everyone has finished both stages.

## Stage 2: choose the sentence

After stage 1 is in, you get a second set of pages. It lists each video you could see, with four English sentences. You may watch the videos again.

Choose which sentence was signed: a, b, c or d. If you are unsure, pick the closest one.

- Judge the signing you see.
- Please do not go back to change stage 1.

## Your code

[Owner's name] gives you a code, for example R07. It goes on your pages. Your name never does.
