# Reviewer guide: judging ASL signing videos

Thank you for reviewing. You may stop at any time. You are paid for the time you spend, including if you stop.

You do not need a computer for this, and you never have to do anything technical. You watch the videos. You give your answers on the printed form, or in ASL to the study team, who write them down and handle everything else.

## What you will see

Your videos use one of two kinds of picture (the study team will tell you which).

**A 3D avatar:** a plain human figure in a dark top, seen from the front, signing. The figure is not a real person. It has short dark hair. Its face is simple: the eyebrows, eyelids and eyes move, and the mouth moves but has no separate lips. Some face signals may be weak or missing, so please tell us when the face looks wrong.

**A simple stick figure** (older studies):
- Top two panels: the upper body from the front and from the side. The signer's left arm is blue and the right arm
  is red.
- Bottom two panels: close-ups of the left and right hand.
- Right-hand panel: a simple face. It shows head turns and nods, eyebrows, eyelids, eye gaze and the mouth. A grey
  outline with no features means the face could not be shown in those frames.

The videos come from different sources, and some may be clearer than others. Please judge each one on its own. You
will not see the same sentence twice.

## For each video

Watch the video as many times as you need. Then answer on that video's page, or in ASL:

| Question | Your answer |
|---|---|
| Could you see it? | If the video does not play, or you cannot judge it at all, tick the box and skip the rest of that page. |
| What was signed? | Your own words, in English or in gloss, whichever is easier. Answer this **before** reading the four sentences. |
| Which sentence? | Which of the four sentences was signed: a, b, c or d. If you are unsure, pick the closest one. |
| What kind of sentence? | Statement, yes/no question, wh-question or rhetorical question |
| Was it negative? | Yes if the signed sentence is negative (for example a headshake or NOT), otherwise no |
| Was a topic set up? | Yes if a topic is set up at the start (for example with raised brows), otherwise no |
| Did it look natural? | 1 = not signing; 2 = hard to follow; 3 = understandable but unnatural; 4 = mostly natural; 5 = fluent and natural |
| Anything else? | What looked wrong, which sign was unclear, or what was missing on the face |

- Judge the signing you see, not the English sentences.
- You may leave videos out; they count as not reviewed.
- Please work alone and do not discuss the videos with other reviewers until everyone has finished.

## Your code

The study team gives you a code, for example R07. It goes on your pages. Your name never does.
